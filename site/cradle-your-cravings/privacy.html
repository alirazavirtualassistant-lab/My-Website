Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: 2026. This is a template policy and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before launch.

Your data is yours

Your assessment results, dashboard, genogram, mood logs, food commits, and any journal entries are encrypted and private. We do not sell user data, ever.

What we collect

Information you provide directly — name, email, password, the data you log in your dashboard — plus limited technical data (cookies, analytics) to improve the product.

How we use it

To run your account, personalize your experience, send Cradle Letter newsletters you opt into, and improve the service.

Payments

Payments are processed by PCI-compliant providers (Stripe / PayPal / Payoneer). We do not store full card details.

Health information

The Trigger Assessment and intake forms collect sensitive health information. It is handled with care and used solely to provide the services you request.

Your choices

Export your data, delete your account, or unsubscribe at any time. Contact cynthiajmm@gmail.com.
