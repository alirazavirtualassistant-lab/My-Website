The methodology

How Cradle Your Cravings works

Four phases drawn from 27 years of practice, the SUGAR® methodology, and Cynthia's peer-reviewed research on food addiction.

Phase 1 · Days 1–7

Understand

You can't change what you can't see. Phase 1 brings everything that's been driving your cravings into focus.

  • ①Take the Hidden Trigger Assessment
  • ②Build your three-generation genogram
  • ③Identify your two specific patterns to interrupt

What you'll see at the end of Phase 1

A personalized map of your relationship with food across four scientifically-grounded profiles, plus the specific generational threads you can choose to end.

Try the assessment ›

The daily craving log

Five seconds, twice a day. Watch your cravings drop 60% on average over the first 14 days.

See the dashboard ›
Phase 2 · Days 8–28

Track

The science is unequivocal: people who log their cravings reduce them faster. Phase 2 makes the invisible visible.

  • ①Log cravings + meals + mood
  • ②The Daily Food Commit ritual
  • ③Weekly check-ins with your recovery circle
Phase 3 · Days 29–56

Transform

Now the deeper work. Your Customized Food Plan emerges from your data. Old patterns get interrupted and replaced with new rituals.

  • ①Receive your Customized Food Plan
  • ②Map the two patterns you'll interrupt
  • ③Build weekly micro-rituals

A note from Cynthia

"Transformation happens in the small daily moments — not the big declarations. Phase 3 is where the work moves from your head into your hands."

— Cynthia Myers-Morrison, EdD

Recovery is a community sport

Phase 4 connects you to a small circle of people walking the same path. Weekly gatherings. Shared rituals. The quiet power of being witnessed.

Meet the circles ›
Phase 4 · Days 57+

Sustain

Recovery isn't a thing you do once. Phase 4 builds the rhythm and community that keeps you held for years.

  • ①Weekly recovery circles
  • ②Family ritual library
  • ③Ongoing access to Cynthia's research updates
