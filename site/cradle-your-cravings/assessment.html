The Hidden Trigger Assessment

Discover what's really driving your cravings — and exactly where to begin. Eight honest questions. A clear, compassionate result.

Why these four profiles

The science behind the assessment

①

Sugar-driven cravings

Maps the classic dopamine-reward profile seen in research on sugar addiction. Built on the SUGAR® methodology Cynthia is licensed to administer.

②

Ultra-processed pull

Grounded in Cynthia's published research on integrating UPF addiction into the management of type 2 diabetes.

③

Emotional eating loop

Draws on trauma-informed approaches (Bessel van der Kolk's work, among others) for understanding eating as a coping pattern.

④

Generational pattern carrier

Informed by Cynthia's research on intergenerational epigenetics and the food environment — and her clinical work with genograms.

The Trigger Assessment is informational and is not a medical diagnosis. Results are for self-understanding only — the full SUGAR® Assessment is administered one-on-one by Cynthia.
