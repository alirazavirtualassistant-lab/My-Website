Who it's for

Designed for the way real recovery happens

Not just for individuals — Cradle holds space for the families and communities healing alongside you.

For individuals

Cravings, food obsession, the diet-binge cycle — the patterns end here. Track triggers, find your profile, and follow a path designed around you.

For families

Especially powerful for those preparing for pregnancy, navigating fertility, or wanting to stop the patterns from being passed forward. Cradle includes whole-family support.

For communities

Practitioners and group leaders can guide recovery circles, churches, sober communities, and support groups using Cradle's shared rituals and tools.

The methodology

Understand. Track. Transform. Sustain.

Four phases drawn from 27 years of practice and the science of food addiction.

01

Understand

Begin with the Trigger Assessment to see exactly what's driving your cravings — sugar chemistry, processed-food pull, emotional patterns, or generational threads.

02

Track

Log cravings, meals, and mood in seconds. Watch the pattern that's been invisible for years come into focus.

03

Transform

Receive a personalized food plan, generational genogram, and weekly micro-rituals that interrupt the patterns and build new ones.

04

Sustain

Hold the change in community — recovery circles, family rituals, and ongoing rhythm that lasts a lifetime.

The science underneath

Built on 27 years of practice and peer-reviewed research

Cradle Your Cravings is grounded in the SUGAR® methodology, the work of the Food Addiction Institute, and Cynthia's published research on ultra-processed food addiction, intergenerational epigenetics, and parental health.

It treats food addiction the way the science actually shows it works — as a treatable brain condition, not a failure of willpower.
