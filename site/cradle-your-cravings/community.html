Recovery circles

Small, warm circles. Weekly gatherings. Quiet power.

Cradle's recovery circles bring four to six people together each week — to share rituals, hold space, and witness one another's progress.

●●●

Personal circles

4–6 members on your same phase, meeting weekly via video. Cynthia provides the structure and the prompts. You bring yourself.

♥

Family rooms

For couples and families working together. Especially powerful for preconception, fertility, and parenting through recovery.

⌂

Community gatherings

Monthly larger gatherings with Cynthia. Open Q&A, recovery stories, themed teachings.

A typical week in a circle

Quiet rhythm, real connection

  • M
    Monday — Set intentions
    A 5-minute prompt in the app. Set this week's quiet intention.
  • W
    Wednesday — Live circle (45 min)
    The heart of the week. Each member shares for 5 minutes, the group reflects, Cynthia closes with a teaching.
  • F
    Friday — Soft witness
    A voice-note or text to a circle partner. Small, gentle, real.
  • S
    Sunday — Reflect & reset
    10-minute journaling prompt. Look at your week. Choose the next one's commitment.
