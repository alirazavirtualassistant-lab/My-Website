The journey

12 weeks that change everything.

Cradle Your Cravings is a 12-week guided journey — and a lifelong practice. Here's exactly what you'll experience.

Week 1

Arrival

Welcome ritual, Trigger Assessment, begin your genogram. Meet your recovery circle.

Week 2

Beginning to see

First week of craving logs. The Daily Food Commit becomes a habit.

Week 3

The patterns surface

Identify your two specific patterns to interrupt. First trend chart appears.

Week 4

Held in community

First full recovery circle gathering. Shared rituals begin.

Week 5–6

Your food plan emerges

Cynthia helps you build a Customized Food Plan from your real data.

Week 7–8

Transformation phase begins

Old patterns interrupted. New micro-rituals built. Sustained-energy weeks.

Week 9–10

Generational repair

Deeper genogram work. Identify which patterns you're ending for the family.

Week 11

Integration

What was practice becomes life. The rituals are yours now.

Week 12

A new threshold

A graduation ritual. Ongoing access continues. Many members deepen with a second cohort.

Your first step is small. The journey is long. Cradle holds you all the way.
