## Why the next 90 days matter

- A sperm cell takes roughly 74 days to develop plus about two weeks to mature (Heller & Clermont, *Science*; summarized by the NIH).
- The follicle that releases an egg spends its final months of growth in a similar window (Gougeon, *Human Reproduction*).
- The 2018 *Lancet* preconception series describes the months before conception as a critical window in which both partners' nutrition and lifestyle shape the pregnancy and the child.
- Epigenetics research (Dutch Hunger Winter, *PNAS* 2008; Överkalix cohort, *European Journal of Human Genetics* 2006) shows that pre-conception conditions can leave marks that reach the next generation.

That is why the course is designed as a 12-week journey: one module roughly every week, with time built in to practise rather than just watch.

## How each training works

1. Watch the video (7–15 minutes). Every script is delivered by Cynthia, first-person, no jargon without an explanation.
2. Download the resource underneath it: a worksheet, recipe set, tracker, guide, or audio.
3. Do the actions (usually two or three). Each one earns XP when you mark it complete.
4. Share in the forum. This is optional for most trainings but it is where the community lives, and it is almost always worth XP.

## Support and safety

This course is education, not medical care. Please involve your doctor, midwife or a registered dietitian before changing supplements, starting an elimination diet, beginning a new exercise programme or trying any detox practice, especially if you have a diagnosed condition, are undergoing fertility treatment, or are already pregnant.
