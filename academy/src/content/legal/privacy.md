## What we collect

- **Account data**: name, email, password (hashed), optional avatar, and sign-in provider (email, magic link, or Google).
- **Purchase data**: what you bought and when. Card details are handled by Stripe and never touch our servers.
- **Learning data**: which lessons you have watched or completed, your progress, XP, badges and streaks.
- **Private learning content**: your quiz and survey answers, journal entries, notes, and files you upload for action steps.
- **Community content**: posts and replies you choose to share, including anonymous posts (anonymity hides your name from other members, not from moderators).
- **Technical data**: standard server logs. If analytics are enabled they are privacy-friendly (Plausible or PostHog, cookie-consent aware) and never run on lesson pages.

## What stays private to you

Your quiz and survey answers, journals, notes and uploads are private. The Instructor and her team can see **that** you completed a step, not **what** you wrote or uploaded, unless you choose to share it (for example by posting in the community). Files you upload are stored in a private bucket that only you (and the server acting for you) can read.

## How we use data

To run the Academy: sign you in, deliver the courses you bought, track progress, award XP and certificates, send transactional emails (receipts, reminders, drip unlocks, certificates), and support you. We do not sell personal data and we do not use advertising trackers.

## Emails

Transactional emails are always sent. Progress nudges, community and newsletter emails can be switched off in Account → Email preferences or via the unsubscribe link.

## Your rights

You can export your data (Account → Data & privacy → Export) and delete your account. Deletion removes your profile, progress, private content and community posts (replaced by "Deleted member"); purchase records are kept as long as required for accounting.

## Processors

Supabase (database, auth, storage), Stripe (payments), Mux (video), Resend (email), Vercel (hosting), and the analytics provider if enabled.
