## Essential cookies

- **Session cookie** — keeps you signed in. Required.
- **Cart** — remembers items in your cart before checkout.
- **Preferences** — theme (light/dark), cookie-consent choice, display currency. Stored in your browser's local storage.

## Analytics (optional)

If enabled, we use a privacy-friendly analytics tool (Plausible, which sets no cookies, or PostHog with cookie consent). Analytics never run on lesson pages. You can decline analytics in the cookie banner and change your choice at any time from the footer link.

## Third parties

Stripe may set cookies during checkout for fraud prevention. Mux may set cookies required to play video.
