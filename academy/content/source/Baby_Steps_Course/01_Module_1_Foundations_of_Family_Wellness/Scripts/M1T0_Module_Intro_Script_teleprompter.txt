— 0:00–0:30  HOOK —

Every tree you have ever admired started underground, in the part nobody photographs. The roots came first, quietly, for a long time, before a single leaf appeared. This module is your roots. And I want you to know something before we dig: the roots you grow over the next few weeks will still be holding your family steady long after the leaves have changed a hundred times.

— 0:30–1:50  WHY THIS MODULE MATTERS —

In 2018, The Lancet published a series on preconception health, and its authors made a point that I wish every couple heard before they ever saw a pregnancy test. The months before conception, they wrote, are a critical window, a time when a mother's and a father's nutrition and lifestyle shape the pregnancy, the child, and possibly the generation after that. Sperm take roughly three months to develop. An egg spends about the same time in its final stretch of maturation before it's released. So the ninety days in front of you are not a waiting room. They are a workshop, and you're the one holding the tools.

In this first module we are not going to overhaul your diet, your exercise, or your bathroom cabinet. That comes later, gently. Here we do the quieter work. We look honestly at where you're starting. We understand why cravings show up and what they're trying to tell you. We learn the mindset that makes change stick instead of snapping back. And we bring your partner in as a teammate rather than a spectator. Foundations first. Everything else in this course sits on top of what we build here.

— 1:50–3:50  WHAT'S INSIDE MODULE 1 —

Training One is your introduction to pre-conception health. You'll meet the five pillars we'll return to again and again, nutrition, movement, stress, toxins, and connection, and you'll take a short self-assessment so you have a baseline. Not a grade. A starting line.

Training Two is about cravings and triggers. If you've ever felt hijacked by a craving and then ashamed afterward, this one is for you. We'll look at the brain chemistry, the connection to your hormones, and a simple three-day tracking method that turns cravings from enemies into information.

Training Three is mindset. We'll talk about perfectionism and doubt, the two barriers I see most often, and you'll get an affirmations audio and a vision-board practice you can start the same day.

Training Four brings in your partner. You'll get a Partner Agreement template and a fifteen-minute meeting plan, and if you're doing this on your own, I'll show you how to build a support circle instead.

And Training Five is our module review: three takeaways, one intention for the week, and a look ahead to nutrition in Module Two.

Each training comes with a resource and two or three simple actions worth XP. Finishing the whole module earns a bonus ten. The Foundations Overview PDF under this video maps it all on one page, so you always know where you are and what's next.
