— 0:30–2:00  WHY INTEGRATION MATTERS —

Here's the honest picture. Sperm are rebuilt every three months. Eggs spend their final months of maturation in a similar window. So the ninety days you've just lived through have already changed the environment in which the cells that could become your child are being built. But biology doesn't stop at day ninety. If conception happens next month, the habits carry straight into pregnancy, where the same nutrition, sleep, stress management, and clean environment matter even more. And if it takes longer, the habits are what carry you through the waiting without the ground giving way. Research on habit formation, Phillippa Lally's work in the European Journal of Social Psychology, found automaticity takes a median of sixty-six days. You're at about sixty-three. This module is where the habits stop being a course and start being a life.

One more reason integration matters, and it's about how people fall away. In my experience, nobody stops because they decided the habits were wrong. They stop because life got loud, the plan wasn't written down anywhere, and three missed days quietly became three months. This module is the written-down part: one page, a log, a dashboard, a set of milestones, and a plan for the loud weeks. It's the least glamorous module and, I think, the most important.
